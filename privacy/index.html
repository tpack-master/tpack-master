Tidy Pack Master

Privacy Policy

PRIVACY

This policy explains, item by item, what Tidy Pack Master keeps on your device while you play, what it uses that for, and how you can clear it. Think of it as the packing list for your save file.

BOX 01What the game keeps on your device

Tidy Pack Master is a puzzle game about packing other people's homes. To let you continue where you left off, the game keeps a small save record in its own storage on your iPhone. This record is created the first time you open the game and is updated as you play.

The save record contains:

  • The rooms you have packed and the number of stars you earned in each one.
  • Your coin balance, and how many of Biscuit's fetches, spare blankets and hints are in your toolbox.
  • The studio decorations you have placed, such as the wall clock or the braided rug.
  • Which client letters you have opened, which Moving Day scenes you have seen, and the total number of boxes you have sealed.
  • Whether the short tutorials on the studio screen and in the first room have been completed.
  • Your settings: music, sound effects and vibration.

The record holds game progress and preferences only. It does not hold your name, your contacts, your photos, your location or anything you type.

BOX 02How the save record is used

The game reads the save record when it starts, so the studio shows your current client, your box stack and any decorations you have placed. The job board reads it to show which rooms are open and how many stars each one has, and the memory album reads it to show the stories your clients have shared.

The record is written when something changes: when you finish a room, spend coins in the toolbox or the decorate page, use a tool, open a new client letter or change a setting. Each setting is read by the part of the game it controls. For example, the music switch is checked before any song plays, and the vibration switch is checked before each little buzz when something lands in a box.

Stars are worked out from how long a room took you, compared with the time shown next to the stars at the top of the packing screen. Coins are added to your balance when a client rewards you for a room, and taken away when you exchange them for a tool or a decoration. Both values are kept in the save record so they are the same the next time you open the game.

The save record is used only to run the game for you. It is not used to build a profile, and it is not combined with information from other apps.

BOX 03Links that open outside the game

A few buttons on the settings page open something outside the game, and only when you tap them:

  • Privacy Policy and Terms of Use open these pages in your web browser. What the browser does with the visit is described by the browser itself.
  • Contact Us opens your mail app with our address filled in. Your message, and the address you send it from, reach us only if you choose to send it.
  • Rate Tidy Pack Master asks the system to show its rating window. Any rating or review you leave is handled by the system and the store it belongs to.

If your mail app cannot be opened, the game copies our email address to your clipboard and shows it on screen, so you can write to us in any way you like.

BOX 04Emails you send us

When you write to us, we use your message and your email address to read your question and reply to it. We keep the conversation for as long as it is useful to help you, for example while we are looking into a problem you reported, and then delete it.

We will only use your email address to answer you. If you would like us to delete an email conversation sooner, just ask in a reply and we will take care of it.

BOX 05Keeping your progress safe

The save record stays in the game's own storage area on your device, which other apps cannot read. It is included in the device backups you make, so restoring a backup to a new iPhone also brings back your studio and your progress.

Because the record lives on your device, it is protected by the same passcode and security settings that protect the rest of your iPhone.

BOX 06Your choices

You are in control of the save record at all times:

  • Change music, sound effects or vibration at any time on the settings page. The change takes effect right away and is remembered.
  • Turn the music off and keep the sound effects, or the other way round. Each switch is saved on its own and is read by the part of the game it controls.
  • Remove all progress by deleting the game from your device. This deletes the save record together with the game. If you install the game again later, you start a fresh studio with the starting coins and tools.
