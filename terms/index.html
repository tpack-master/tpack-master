BOX 02The game

In each room you pick up things that nothing else is lying on and pack them into the three open boxes, each of which asks for three of one kind. Anything no box wants yet waits on a small tray, and a full tray means the room is packed again from the start. Clients reward you with coins when a room is packed, and tell you a little story about the things you packed. Each client brings something new, such as bundles wrapped in newspaper, taped cartons that pop open, vines that need clearing, and big gear that takes two tray spaces.

The game is provided for personal entertainment. We may add new clients, rooms and features, adjust puzzles to make them fairer, or change how parts of the game work. Your saved progress is kept whenever we can.

BOX 03Coins, tools and decorations

Coins are earned by packing rooms. You can spend them in the toolbox on Biscuit's fetches, spare blankets and hints, or on decorations for your studio. Some details:

  • Coins, tools and decorations exist only inside the game. They cannot be transferred to another person or exchanged for anything outside the game.
  • Tools are used up when you use them in a room. Leaving a room or starting it over does not bring a used tool back.
  • Prices and rewards inside the game may be rebalanced so the game stays fun and fair.

BOX 04Your progress

Your progress is saved on your device, as described in our Privacy Policy. Deleting the game removes the saved progress, and it cannot be restored by us. Restoring a device backup brings it back together with the rest of your backup.

Rooms you have already packed stay open on the job board, so you can return to any of them to try for three stars. Replaying a room never lowers the stars you have already earned for it.
